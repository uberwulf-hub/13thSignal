## New
- The facility notices you:
  - Run or clang across a grate, and the nearest intercom answers with a clipped chime (in Survivor and Nightmare, MERIDIAN adds "...noted.")
  - Lights go out behind you one by one, and come back later
  - A room's name on the floor sometimes reads SECTOR 13 while you aren't looking
  - The elevator display shows B13 for a moment the first time you get close
- ECHO plays back your own footsteps from where you made them, plus one you didn't
- In Sector 6, the mannequins turn their heads a little while you look away, and after you reach the stockroom they all turn to face you
- In Sector 3, a lab goes dark for one beat with a deep thump when you solve it
- New in Survivor and Nightmare:
  - The Listener goes silent as it closes in on a noise, then breathes close by
  - False footsteps in a side corridor, then a pipe taps twice in the same spot
  - MERIDIAN might say something only ECHO has said
