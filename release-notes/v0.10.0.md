## New
- The game now tells a story across a whole run:
  - Every sector hides one record about the 13th sector, somewhere new each game. The game counts how many you find
  - MERIDIAN changes as you go down: steady at first, then it starts pausing and correcting itself, then (outside Explorer) it borrows ECHO's words. In Sector 6, it admits its map is incomplete
  - Three notes first heard in the relays keep coming back. ECHO hums them, and the showroom plays them
  - At Sector 6's elevator, you choose what gets the last spare line of power: the ROUTE MAP (it shows a 13th sector) or the CAB LIGHTS (a calm ride down). Each changes MERIDIAN's last line
  - As the doors close, the elevator shows your route, and in Survivor and Nightmare "INCOMPLETE [13]" if you found 4 or more records. Then the three notes play once more, with one missing
