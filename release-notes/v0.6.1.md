## New
- Added fullscreen and windowed modes: switch with F11, Alt+Enter, or Options → Display. The game starts in fullscreen
- Added a real flashlight model in your left hand, and real models for the wrench, clipboard and stapler
- Redesigned the signal fragments as dark, glowing crystals that hum and glitch
- Made mannequins solid: you can't walk through them, and thrown things bounce off with a hollow knock... and then something inside them moves
- Made thrown items bounce realistically, with a sound for every hit: one for the item itself and one for what it hit (concrete, grates, metal doors, glass, mannequins)

## Fixed
- Fixed the menu getting cut off: it now fits any window size and scrolls if the window is very small
- Made an item's description show up only the first time you read it. After that, a short reminder points to the journal
- Aimed the flashlight beam at the crosshair
- Made the journal scroll (mouse wheel, arrow keys, or drag on phones) and fit the screen when it gets long
- Made glass and mugs break only when they hit hard
