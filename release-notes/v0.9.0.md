## New
- Monsters start somewhere different every game (from v0.8.2)
- Gave each sector its own lighting:
  - Archives: tired orange lamps that slowly swell and fade
  - Relay station: lights that drift between red and blue
  - Security: cold white light with a slow scanning wave
  - Playback lab: sickly green
  - Showroom: warm light that dies for a moment now and then
- Made the halls feel used: cable conduits, vents, warning plates, service panels and pipes along the walls, water streaks, grime where walls meet the floor, and old stains
- Added dust in the air that you only see in your flashlight beam
- Made the flashlight beam softer at the edges, with a faint outer glow. A weak battery makes it shorter, dimmer and yellower, and below 20% it wavers and drops out
- Added a slow breathing sway to the camera when you stand still
