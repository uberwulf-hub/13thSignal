## New
- Added realistic footsteps: heel and toe with grit underfoot, many variations, and different sounds for crouching, walking and running
- Added metal grates that clang, ring and rattle under your feet
- Added the sound of the mannequin following you: hollow plastic steps, creaking joints and a dragging foot, only while you aren't looking
- Added a concrete-room echo to sounds in the world, and far-away sounds now come through muffled
- Added 12 mannequin poses with working elbows, like thinking, hands on hips, arms crossed and waving (the unsettling ones only in Survivor and Nightmare)
- Removed the markings from the mannequin's face: it's a smooth blank head now
