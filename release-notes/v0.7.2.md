## Changed
- Made the Listener much harder to shake off:
  - Once it's hunting you, it keeps going longer after your last sound, and hears your footsteps from further away
  - When it loses you, it searches the spots around where it last heard you before it gives up
  - Its long arms reach a little further when it grabs
- Made the Listener tougher in Explorer too:
  - It senses you if you stand right next to it while it listens
  - It heads for where you're going, and gets hunches about where you are more often
  - Explorer still has no jump scare unless it's switched on, and the Listener still never learns to check lockers
