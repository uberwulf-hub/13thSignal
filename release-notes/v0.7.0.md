## New
- Rebuilt the service elevator to look the part: brushed-steel doors that slide apart, a work lamp over them, and a real cab inside with handrails, a button panel and a buzzing halogen light. One of the buttons says 13
- Added an ending cutscene for every sector: the camera pulls out of your head until you see yourself in the elevator, the doors close, the light flickers and buzzes, and the screen goes black
- Your items now ride the elevator with you into the next sector
- Made the Listener scarier:
  - It hears you run: it rears up, screeches, and comes for the sound fast
  - When it catches you, you fall backwards and it bends down over you, face to face. In Survivor and Nightmare its face is suddenly right in yours, with a shriek
  - Its glitch tears the picture and leaves black smears of itself behind, instead of TV static
- Added a Parent Settings option (behind the PIN) to turn on the full catch scare in Explorer. It's off by default
- Restyled every menu and panel: a worn typewriter font on stained, chipped panels
- Made everything you drop or throw bounce, with realistic sounds for what it is and what it hits
- Made mannequins solid

## Fixed
- Fixed room names on the floor getting cut off in narrow halls (like the SERVICE SHAFT in Sector 2)
- Made the Listener in Explorer hear you a little further away and move a little faster, so running around carelessly isn't safe any more
