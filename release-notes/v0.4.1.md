## New
- Added loud doors: opening one is a bang the Listener can hear from far away, and the clank as it shuts gives away where you were
- Added crawl-learning: crouch-walk too much and the Listener learns to hear you (not in Explorer)
- Added a less predictable Listener: random pauses, hunches about where you are, and lunges when it hunts
- Added close-range sensing: when the Listener stops to listen, it can sense you nearby even if you're silent (not in Explorer)
- Added the stare glitch: look at the Listener too long and it glitches out and reappears a few metres away
- Crouching is now quieter, not silent
