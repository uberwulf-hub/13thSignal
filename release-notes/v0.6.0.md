## New
- Added an inventory: six slots along the bottom, picked with 1-6 or the scroll wheel (tap a slot on phones)
- Added your hands: the flashlight in your left, and whatever you've picked in your right
- Added everyday items in every sector, in different places each game
- Added throwing: toss a can, bottle or wrench and the Listener goes to check where it landed, not where you are
- Added batteries that recharge your flashlight
- Added glow sticks: crack one and toss it, and mannequins can't move in its light
- Added junk you can still throw as a distraction (mugs and bottles break), plus a photo and a badge with story scraps
- Added the ITEM button on phones, and controller support (right trigger to use, D-pad to choose and drop)
